    In the Library

    Key What it does
    / Ctrl+f Find a book, or words in every book
    h j k l, arrows Move between books, and down into the words found
    Enter, double-click Open; a single click opens too when Options sets Library click to Open
    b v Shelf or List
    L Keys into the outline and back
    cc Outline as chapters, or with their parts
    B o Close the Library
    L from a place found That book's outline, from the first chapter holding the words; L again comes back to the place
    Esc One step back each time: cancel Remove all; leave the outline; from a book or a place found to the find box; clear the find; leave the box; then close
